Troubleshooting & Tips

Common issues and simple fixes

Quick Fixes:

❌ Won't Mix Properly
• Add ingredients to water, not water to ingredients
• Shake in bursts: 10 seconds, pause, repeat
😵 Too Sweet
• Next time: Use 1 fewer green sugar scoop
🤢 Stomach Issues
• Try "Easy Morning" recipe first
• Reduce by 1 green scoop each
😐 Tastes Bland
• Add 1 more purple flavor scoop

Customization Tips:

  • Start conservative - easier to add than subtract
  • Adjust one scoop at a time between rides
  • Hot weather? Always add 1 extra purple scoop
  • Long rides? Test higher concentrations first

Storage & Safety:

  • Keep bags sealed and dry
  • Clean scoops after use
  • Use within 6 months
Remember: Every athlete is different. These are starting points - adjust based on your body's response and experience.
